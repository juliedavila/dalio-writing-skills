# Dalio-style writing skills

Three Claude skills for writing work documents in the style of Ray Dalio: layered (nutshell first), mechanism before cases, explicit confidence and odds, named owners, and data presented as sourced gauges. They also add guardrails against the weaknesses critics point out in his writing: bullet-heavy "PowerPoint" structure, platitudes, cherry-picked templates, unsourced charts, and person-level blame.

| Skill | Use it for |
|---|---|
| [`dalio-decision-memo`](dalio-decision-memo/SKILL.md) | Decision memos and decision-meeting pre-reads |
| [`dalio-strategy-analysis`](dalio-strategy-analysis/SKILL.md) | Strategy and planning docs; recurring status and risk reports |
| [`dalio-diagnosis-principles`](dalio-diagnosis-principles/SKILL.md) | Postmortems and diagnoses (blameless by default); team operating principles |

## Install

Copy a skill folder into your skills directory (for Claude Code, `~/.claude/skills/`), or upload the folder as a skill in Claude.

## Basis

The skills come from research into Dalio's *Principles*, *Big Debt Crises*, *The Changing World Order* and *How Countries Go Broke*, his essays, the documented Bridgewater protocols, and third-party critiques. Techniques with no source (for example "1-2-3 go") are deliberately not attributed to him.
